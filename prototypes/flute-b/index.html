A 30-minute lesson

What happens in a lesson

  1. 5 min

    Warm-up

    Breathing games with soap bubbles and feathers.

  2. 10 min

    Something new

    A new note or rhythm, one step at a time.

  3. 10 min

    Favourite song

    We play together, often as a duet with the teacher.

  4. 5 min

    Practice plan & sticker

    A short plan for home, and a sticker as a reward.
