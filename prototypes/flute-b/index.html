Flute lessons

One-to-one lessons for every age and level

From the very first note to advanced repertoire. Choose the lesson length that suits you.

30min

A focused lesson, ideal for young children and beginners. Small children can start on a flute with a curved headjoint, so short arms reach the keys.

45min

More time for tone, technique, reading music and new pieces.

60min

In-depth work on repertoire, chamber music and baroque style, for advanced students and adults.

Your teacher

Hi, I'm Suhsuan!

I'm Suhsuan Wang, a flautist from Taiwan. I studied at the Hochschule für Musik und Tanz Köln, where I completed my Bachelor's in flute performance and my Master's in flute performance, chamber music and baroque music.

Today I teach students of all ages in Bonn, Köln and online. In my lessons, laughing, trying things out and the odd wrong note are all welcome. That's how the joy of playing grows.
