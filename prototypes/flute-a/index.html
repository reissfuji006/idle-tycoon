FAQ

Questions parents often ask

How old does my child need to be?

Flute lessons start from about age 5, with a recorder or a flute with a curved headjoint that is easier for small hands to hold. Early childhood music starts at age 3.

Do we need an instrument already?

Not for the trial lesson. Afterwards I'm happy to advise you on buying or renting one.

How much does my child need to practise?

At the start, 10 minutes on most days is enough. Every child gets a short practice plan.

Which language are lessons in?

English, German or Mandarin, whichever your child is most comfortable with. Bilingual lessons are welcome too.
