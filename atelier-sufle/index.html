About

Suhsuan Wang, flautist and teacher

Suhsuan Wang is a flautist from Taiwan, based between Bonn and Köln. She studied at the Hochschule für Musik und Tanz Köln, where she earned a Bachelor of Music in flute performance and a Master of Music in flute performance, chamber music and baroque music.

Every lesson is one-to-one and shaped around the student: their age, their level and the music they want to play. Lessons are given in English, German or Chinese.

Lessons

One-to-one flute lessons

For complete beginners, returning players and advanced flautists. Lessons follow each student’s age, level and goals.

Who the lessons are for

Children
Young beginners can start on a flute with a curved headjoint, so short arms reach the keys comfortably.
Teenagers
Tone, technique, reading music and repertoire, at their own pace.
Adults
First steps, a return to the flute after years away, or advanced work on repertoire.

Lesson length

30 min
A focused lesson, ideal for young children and first steps.
45 min
More time for tone, technique and new pieces.
60 min
In-depth work on repertoire, chamber music and baroque style.
