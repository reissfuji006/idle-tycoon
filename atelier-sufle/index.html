About

Suhsuan Wang, flautist and teacher

Suhsuan Wang is a flautist from Taiwan, based between Bonn and Köln. She trained at the Hochschule für Musik und Tanz Köln, specialising in flute performance, chamber music and baroque music, and now teaches students of every age in English, German or Chinese.
